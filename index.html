YT Factory

A private publishing tool for the owner's own YouTube channels.

YT Factory is a personal automation tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used by that one person to publish and manage videos on YouTube channels they own. It is not a public service: there are no user accounts, nothing to sign up for, and it is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's YouTube channels as private videos, with a scheduled publish time the owner can change or cancel in YouTube Studio.
  • Updates the title, description or thumbnail of the owner's own videos, and replaces a video that was uploaded by mistake before it is published.
  • Reads the YouTube Analytics of the owner's own channels (views, watch time, traffic sources) so the owner can see which videos work.
  • Reads and writes the owner's own Google Sheet, which serves as the tool's to-do list and control panel.
  • Sends the owner a daily summary email, from the owner's Gmail account to the owner.

Why it signs in with Google

Each of these actions uses an official Google API, and Google requires the owner to approve access once with their own account. The access is used only for the actions listed above, only on the owner's own channels, sheet and mailbox. How the data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.

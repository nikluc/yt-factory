Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what data YT Factory ("the app") accesses through Google sign-in, why, and how it is stored. The app is a private tool used by one person, its owner, on their own computer, to manage their own YouTube channels. It has no other users.

Data the app accesses

The app asks for the following Google permissions, and uses each one only as described:

PermissionWhat the app does with it
YouTube: upload videos
youtube.upload		 Uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's own channels, as private videos with a scheduled publish time.
YouTube: view account
youtube.readonly		 Checks which channel is signed in, so a video never goes to the wrong channel, and reads the status and schedule of the owner's own videos.
YouTube: manage videos
youtube.force-ssl		 Edits the title, description or thumbnail of the owner's own videos, and deletes a mistaken upload of the owner's own that has not been published yet.
YouTube Analytics: view reports
yt-analytics.readonly		 Reads view counts, watch time, retention and traffic sources for the owner's own channels.
Google Sheets
spreadsheets		 Reads and writes the owner's own planning spreadsheet (video ideas, scripts, schedule, status).
Gmail: send email
gmail.send		 Sends one daily summary email from the owner's account to the owner. The app does not read, search or delete any email.

How the data is stored

  • The sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local folder that is never uploaded or shared.
  • Analytics numbers are stored in a local database on the same computer, for the owner's own reports.
  • The app has no server and no cloud database of its own. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the Google APIs listed above, to perform the action the owner asked for.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. It does not use Google user data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. No person other than the owner can see the data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner is also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of the data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through the Google security settings page.
  • Withdrawing access stops the app immediately. The locally stored tokens and analytics can then be deleted by removing the app's folder from the owner's computer; nothing is kept anywhere else.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the issue page of this site's repository.
